# Project Overview

## Project Name
DreamMore Platform

## Purpose and Business Goals
DreamMore is a digital platform designed to support a modern service and education business. It combines:
- a marketing and agency website for showcasing services and portfolio
- an academy section for online courses and learning content
- an LMS experience for course delivery and student progress tracking
- admin tools for managing content, users, applications, and payments

The business goals include increasing lead generation, simplifying course enrollment, improving student engagement, and enabling internal operations through a centralized platform.

## Main Features
- Public-facing marketing pages for home, about, agency, academy, and contact
- Course catalog and course detail pages
- Course application workflow for student enrollment
- Authentication and account management for users and admins
- Blog, portfolio, testimonials, trusted brands, and team sections
- Admin dashboard for content and operational management
- File upload integration for images, notes, and payments
- Email-based verification and password reset flows

## Target Users
- Prospective clients seeking digital services
- Students and learners enrolling in courses
- Instructors and LMS content managers
- Administrators managing platform content and user activity
